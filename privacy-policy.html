Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GolbertOps ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the use of automation tools for publishing content on LinkedIn and managing cloud engineering workflows.

1. Information We Collect

Our automated applications do not collect personal data from end-users. We strictly access proprietary corporate information: credentials for accessing the LinkedIn API (tokens), content generated for publications, and data pertaining to the organization's account being managed.

2. Use of Information

The information collected is used exclusively to:

  • Publish content on the organization's LinkedIn profile.
  • Manage and schedule automated publications.
  • Maintain the security of access credentials.

3. Storage and Security

Access tokens and credentials are securely stored and are not shared with third parties. We do not sell, rent, or lease personal or corporate data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Data

We utilize official APIs (including the LinkedIn API Program) and artificial intelligence services (such as Groq). Data processing by these services is governed by their respective privacy policies: LinkedIn Privacy Policy · Groq Privacy Policy.

5. User Rights

If you have any questions regarding this policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us via email at customers@golbert.cloud or through our corporate LinkedIn profile.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page.
